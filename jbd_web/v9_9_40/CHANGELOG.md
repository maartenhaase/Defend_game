# V9.9.40 — Cover tactics, component damage and WWII support

Enemy infantry now have about 38% more health than V9.9.39, with a 5.4 progression multiplier cap. Severe small-arms wounds occur below 28% health instead of automatically removing a soldier after a large first hit. Enemy squads seek the protected side of nearby usable cover, avoid overcrowding and fire, hold positions for 2.4–4.2 seconds, and use building corners to keep sight lines. Protected enemies take 56% small-arms damage. Existing horizontal evasions and ten-second central recovery remain. Allied infantry health and medic treatment remain normalized; veteran reinforcements use period weapons rather than body armor.

Ground vehicle health increases about 32% over V9.9.39. A complete motor vehicle survives one well-placed player grenade, with visible mobility/radiator damage; the second well-placed grenade destroys it. Grazing splash chips the hull but does not count as a full direct hit. Direct contact and the same grenade's blast share a per-projectile contact ledger, preventing double damage and duplicate kill credit. Fuel cook-off and powerful artillery can still destroy a vehicle separately. Cavalry remains vulnerable to bullets.

Penetrating impacts independently accumulate damage to tyres, radiator, glass, fuel, body and frame, influenced by impact position. Flat tyres reduce wheeled speed to 30%, then 18%; tracked damage reduces speed to 55%. Radiator failure emits white steam and reduces speed without becoming fuel fire. Shattered glass creates steering sway. Fuel hits retain the gradual leak, spreading puddle, subsequent-hit ignition and visible burn before cook-off. Repeated body hits eject one actual remaining passenger; frame strikes briefly jolt the chassis without arbitrarily damaging the fuel system. Driver injury now requires several penetrating cab/glass hits, rather than any hull hit.

All 40 campaign plans now vary vehicle families, spacing, routes and infantry tactics. An old spawn filter that silently converted every scout, motorcycle and cavalry unit into a truck or halftrack has been removed. All nine actual vehicle-family spawns are regression-tested. Nine families appear in actual plans, including sidecar motorcycles, cavalry and the restored light mobility silhouette redrawn as an Sd.Kfz.2 Kettenkrad. Modern ATV identities and shapes are removed from active campaign selection. Former helicopter encounters use period troop transports. Early-war vehicle naming is adjusted to the period.

The five strike tiers are mortars, a period aircraft bombing run, a short strafing pass, howitzer battery and regimental barrage. Eastern Front maps from 1941 use a 16-round BM-13 Katyusha salvo at the top tier. Aircraft include PZL.23, Hurricane, P-40, Il-2 and P-47 according to battle year/front; P-47 is not offered before 1943. Aircraft fly straight passes and depart. Allied scout/tank rewards use period identities and new coherent artwork, including wz.34/7TP, Dingo/Matilda II, BA-20 or BA-64/T-34, and M3/M4 variants. No Apache or allied Tiger appears in the active ladders. These are period-appropriate game rewards, not a claim to reproduce each battle's exact order of battle.

The upgrade sandbox retains +8 kills and adds ANDER FRONT to cycle Poland, Normandy, Eastern Front and Pacific support sets. Both activation buttons show the current actual reward.

Verification: JavaScript syntax and the complete battle regression suite pass. Coverage includes all ten ladder UI activations, real bombing/strafing projectiles at three screen widths, forty map geometries and plans, 120 difficulty/map parameter combinations, protected-side cover movement and firing, actual bullet survival, independent component effects, passenger accounting, first/second grenade destruction and same-projectile deduplication. Existing manual input, audio, gore, faces, medics, unlimited range, per-surface penetration, fuel ignition, routing and 90-second scout persistence tests pass. A representative early rifleman now takes four direct MG hits versus three previously. Updated vehicle artwork was rendered and visually reviewed. No physical iPhone playtest was performed.
