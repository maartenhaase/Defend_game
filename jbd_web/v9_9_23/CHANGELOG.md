# V9.9.23 — Tiger route and firing cycle fix

- Removes the unavailable `freeLanding` reference and uses Tiger-local obstacle checks for rocks, buildings, solid cover, and wrecks.
- Tiger combat follows a readable cycle: drive out, fire two longer five-round MG bursts, fire one HE shell, drive to a second position and stop for one five-round MG burst, then drive back to the loop start.
- Keeps a brief reverse and reroute when an obstacle blocks the Tiger.
- Ordinary MG rounds mostly ricochet from Tiger armor; explosive hits still damage it.
- Reduces truck fuel-blast radius and strength only.
- Adds a cache-busted page for this release.
