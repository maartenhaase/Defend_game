# V9.9.35 — Four upgrades, unrestricted reach and recovery routes

Four paid upgrade tracks, each with 20 levels: speed + penetration; fire control (reload, magazine and fire rate together); grenade power (damage, slightly faster grenade recharge, 2 slots at level 7 and 3 at level 14); base armor. Old spent points are conserved when combining saved tracks. Bullet damage is fixed. The shop displays actual projectile velocity and all combined stat changes.

MG rounds continue past the aim point to the battlefield edge. Grenades can land at any aimed point on the battlefield. Faster warm ballistic streaks display velocity changes. Baseline penetration is 30%, rising to 82% at level 20 before material resistance; rounds have a finite surface/target budget and can strike multiple aligned enemies. Collision substeps prevent fast bullets skipping targets.

Vehicle gunfire requires multiple successful penetrations to kill the driver (four for an unarmored class-1 vehicle; armored vehicles require more). Internal hull/armor damage increases with penetration upgrades, but small arms cannot explode the hull or ignite it. The driverless vehicle remains intact, receives no premature destruction credit, and can be finished with a grenade. Grenade impacts now apply real vehicle splash damage. A grenade crossing a soldier high overhead does not detonate on that soldier prematurely. Artillery, vehicle cannons and other explosive support retain their anti-vehicle roles.

The enemy movement watchdog persists across AI state changes. After ten seconds without useful progress, infantry requests a fresh route toward the center using solid-building/obstacle geometry, clears stale navigation and fires real suppressive rounds toward the bunker while moving. Routes are refreshed if blocked again. Active stuck vehicles also receive a central route and fire during recovery. Impassable building overlaps/gaps are removed when a map is built. Injured, burning, parachuting and treating-medics retain their dedicated behavior.

Verification: JavaScript syntax; full existing combat/audio, ally, support-ladder, Apache, 40-map and penetration suite; four actual purchase buttons and merged stat effects; old-point migration; both weapon types at portrait and landscape screen edges; baseline cover penetration and fast multi-target hits; repeated vehicle penetration and driver physics; intact driverless hull and one-credit grenade finish; grenade altitude/splash; ten-second counter across AI states, real bunker-directed firing, a solid-house detour, actual vehicle recovery/fire and viable center routes on all 40 campaign maps.
