# V9.9.21 — Tiger Patrol, Armor, and HE Cadence

- Slowed the Tiger's arrival and gave it short roaming waypoints with building avoidance and a brief reverse/re-route when blocked.

- Tiger alternates three-round MG bursts using the MG34 sample with an HE cannon shell that travels to impact and damages nearby enemies.
- Reduced the damage radius and blast strength for truck fuel explosions only.
- Kept the Tiger demo activation flow and enemy waves intact.
