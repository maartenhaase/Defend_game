# V9.9.4 audio credits

V9.9.4 preserves the local CC0 field mix introduced in V9.9.3.

- Firearm recordings: The Free Firearm Sound Library, via the public Fatal Funnel asset pack.
  https://github.com/euuuuuuan/fatal-funnel-public/tree/main/apps/game/public/sfx
- Material impacts: Kenney Impact Sounds, CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/GeorgeQLe/assets-sfx-combat/tree/main/assets/kenney/impact-sounds

All samples remain embedded in `game.js`; gameplay has no third-party audio-host dependency.
