# V9.9.4 — Field Event Update

## New random bonus system

Every 15 player kills awards one random one-shot field event. The event is stored until the player presses **ACTIVEREN**. Queued events are preserved between fixed-map levels.

- Napalm Strike — six marked canisters across the aimed area.
- Artillery Strike — eight warning markers followed by a radial barrage.
- Allied Para Drop — five vulnerable British soldiers parachute onto the middle line.
- Rambo — a unique high-HP M60 gunner with custom hair/bandanna visuals; fights until killed.
- Elite Tiger Tank — unique allied heavy armor with custom hull/turret rendering and a cannon.

Removed from gameplay:

- Rampage meter and ten-second Rampage mode.
- Temporary combat buffs.
- In-battle perk selection modal.
- Automatic kill-based allied vehicle/reinforcement rewards.

## Demo update

The demo automatically showcases all five new field events in its opening 30 seconds. The same five events are also preloaded into the ACTIVEREN queue for manual testing.

## Preserved

Fixed classic maps, road clearing, local CC0 audio mix, one-tap/one-salvo input, hold-for-grenade input, upgradeable grenades, roofless buildings, fire avoidance, vulnerable allied units and enemy trucks/halftracks only.
