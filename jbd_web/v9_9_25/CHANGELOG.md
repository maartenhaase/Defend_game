# V9.9.25 — Two spend-or-save support ladders

- Replaces random bonus drops with a shared bank: every 15 kills unlocks the next rung; higher rungs cost 30, 45, 60, and 75 banked kills. Activate the current rung or keep saving for a stronger reward.
- Adds visible five-step Strike and Reinforcements ladders, selectable with one tap and launched with the existing Activate button.
- Strike rewards: small artillery, plane napalm bombardment, a 30-second AH-64-style helicopter, heavy artillery, and a big precision strike.
- Reinforcement rewards: 3 infantry, a 5-soldier MG command group, 8 elite troopers, an armored MG scout car, and the Tiger.
- Rambo is removed from the reward ladder; the MG command group fills that battlefield role.
- Adds a live 30-second helicopter pass, plane flyby, tier-specific artillery impacts, and stronger elite infantry.
