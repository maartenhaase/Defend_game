# V9.9.33 — SPR ballistics and campaign progression

**SPR** is the single upgrade for projectile speed, firing range, and penetration. Each level increases the chance and number of penetrations. Successful rounds can pass through trees, rock, wrecks, building walls and other cover, then continue through multiple infantry. An armor penetration applies additional damage to the vehicle behind the plate. Existing saves retain their range upgrade level as the SPR level.

Hard campaign pressure now rises over six stages across all 40 maps. The first map starts with a small infantry group and fewer truck passengers; Hard enemy health, damage, accuracy and firing pace ramp up through the campaign. Helicopter landings carry progressively larger infantry groups, and vehicle support grows in later stages.
