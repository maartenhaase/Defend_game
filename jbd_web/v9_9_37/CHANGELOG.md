# V9.9.37 — Tap-to-command area fire, mortar timing and staged vehicle fuel ignition

A short tap now selects an area instead of firing a single immediate round. The selected area and a tapped building glow for one second. The gunner acquires the first area for one second, then fires the remaining magazine inside that circle with the finger released. A new area interrupts the current burst and adds traverse time based on angle, target distance and Fire control skill. Emptying the magazine plays a distinct mechanical empty cue, ends that command and reloads automatically. Firing resumes only after a new tap, including a new command issued during reload.

Fire control remains one of four paid upgrade tracks and now also improves aim/stability. Scatter decreases with upgrade level and settles during a magazine. Level 8 unlocks infantry/vehicle selection within the selected area; level 14 unlocks tracking and lead inside that area. The old unconditional touch snap is bypassed for commanded fire. AI cannot select or follow a target outside the circle. The shop shows the new stability effects and milestones. A spoken radio-style Fire cue plays once when each command actually starts firing.

A hold (350 ms or longer) retains the grenade gesture, reserves one grenade slot and launches the grenade after a further 1.5 seconds. The original unlimited-range arc and grenade upgrade effects remain. Three upgraded slots can reserve three separate mortars without spending another slot twice. Launch uses a distinct mortar report. The Fire, empty-magazine and mortar cues are embedded WAV samples, preloaded through Howler; the new cues require no external audio fetch.

Successful bullet penetration can progressively damage vehicle fuel systems again. Several penetrations first cause a leak; the oil puddle spreads visibly for at least 3.5 seconds before subsequent penetrating shots can ignite it. Fire then intensifies over 6.5–9.5 seconds before fuel cook-off. Failed penetrations do not randomly ignite a hull, and ordinary bullet HP damage still does not directly explode it. Grenades and other explosives retain immediate anti-vehicle damage. The same leak/burn update supports allied hulls; intact wrecks may also be ignited by penetrating fire. Driverless hull labels now reflect intact, leaking or burning state.

Verification: full prior combat/audio, support, scout, forty-map, upgrade, unlimited-range, sequential-penetration, driver, grenade and route suite. New actual-pointer tests at 320x844, 390x844 and 844x390 verify no shot on touch-down, one-second acquisition/glow, exact remaining-magazine firing, sound timing, no automatic fire after reload, command interruption/traverse delay, cancellation and a new command after reload. Mortar tests cover three reservations, 1.5-second timing, correct slot consumption and report. Aim tests compare physical hits and accuracy at equal ammunition, enforce circle boundaries and release targets that leave. Fuel tests verify delayed puddle growth, further-hit ignition, a visible burn period and one-credit destruction. All new WAV cues were checked for valid mono PCM, duration and non-silent content.
