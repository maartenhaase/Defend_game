# V9.9.36 — Visible scout support and sequential penetration

Fixed the allied support scout's missing steering phase. Without it, its horizontal position became NaN on the first movement frame, making an otherwise living vehicle invisible. The scout now initializes all movement values, and the shared allied vehicle update initializes a missing steering phase defensively. It has no short lifetime; normal incoming damage can still destroy it and leaves a wreck. Health and armor stay at their previous values.

Speed + penetration now starts at 30% base penetration and reaches a hard 85% cap at level 20. There is no fixed target/surface count limit. A round checks every contacted surface or target in physical order and stops at its first failed check. All existing solid cover categories, world-solid objects, trees, rocks, wrecks, live vehicles, infantry, aircraft, parachutists and optional targets use this path. Each exterior face of a building is independent: entry and exit walls require separate checks, while adjacent visual parts of the same wall never cause duplicate rolls.

Material resistance and remaining projectile energy determine each effective chance. Successful penetration reduces the round's energy, damage and speed before the next object. The per-round history records the actual chance, random roll and result for every new contact, and passed objects are never repeatedly damaged while the same round exits them. The shop displays the 85% cap and the per-wall/target rule. Existing multi-hit driver damage, grenade finishing, unlimited weapon reach, four upgrade tracks, 40 maps and ten-second enemy recovery remain available.

Verification: all previous combat/audio, support, campaign, upgrade, ballistics, vehicle, grenade and route regressions; actual UI-summoned scouts fighting for 90 seconds at 320/390/844-pixel viewports; finite scout movement on all 40 map geometries and a wreck after destruction; separate entry/exit wall checks with no duplicate wall-segment roll; interleaved wood/infantry/tree/stone/armored vehicle/world-solid metal/wreck collision order; eight-object chain with no count cap; energy and speed attenuation; stop at the first failed obstacle; correct damage to an infantry target in front of a wall; enforced 85% maximum even for invalid 100% input.
