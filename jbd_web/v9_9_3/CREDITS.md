- Source: https://github.com/euuuuuuan/fatal-funnel-public/tree/main/apps/game/public/sfx

## Material impact recordings

- Kenney Impact Sounds.
- Embedded variants: generic ground, soft body/soil, metal, wood, glass, heavy plate, and stone/mining impacts; three takes per material.
- Source mirror: https://github.com/GeorgeQLe/assets-sfx-combat/tree/main/assets/kenney/impact-sounds
- Original publisher: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- License: CC0 1.0.

The samples are embedded as data URIs so gameplay does not depend on third-party audio hosts at runtime.
